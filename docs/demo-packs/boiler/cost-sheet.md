# Boiler room: cost sheet

Riverside Works finance office · Cost rates for the boiler room · Financial year 2026–27, issued 1 Oct 2026

All figures are in Indian rupees and exclude GST. They are the rates the works uses to cost the boiler room's shortfalls and maintenance.

## Rates

Value of a batch not delivered: ₹1,800 per batch.

When the boiler room falls short, the works raises the missing steam on the diesel standby boiler, which costs this much more per batch than the main boilers.

Cost of a rejected batch: ₹650 per batch.

A rejected batch throws away its treated feedwater and the gas burned to raise it.

Maintenance technician, loaded cost: ₹600 per hour.

The loaded cost covers wages, allowances, overtime cover and the tools van.

Spares and consumables per breakdown repair: ₹1,200 per repair on average.

The average is taken over last year's breakdown work orders in the boiler room: seals, gaskets, igniters, fuses and sensor parts.

## Not included

Preventive services use the technician's time only; their parts are bought under the annual service contract and are not charged per job.
