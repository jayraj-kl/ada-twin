# Boilers: datasheet

Tollan Thermal · Model TT-650 packaged fire-tube steam boiler, gas fired · Datasheet DS-TT650, revision B

## Duty

Packaged three-pass fire-tube boiler raising feedwater to saturated steam at 10 bar, two boilers installed.

## Performance per batch

Rated duty: one 500 L batch raised to steam per firing cycle.

Cycle time: 170 s per batch.

Cycle-time variation (standard deviation divided by mean): 0.25.

Batches rejected for wet steam or low pressure: 1% of batches.

## Notes

The cycle time covers purge, firing and release of one batch. Each boiler fires only while gas is available from the valve trains.
