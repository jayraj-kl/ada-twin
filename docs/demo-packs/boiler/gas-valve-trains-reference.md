# Gas valve trains: reference sheet

Published reliability figures for the equipment class. This sheet describes a class of equipment, not this plant.

## Equipment class

Fuel gas valve train for a burner: isolation valve, pressure regulator, two automatic safety shut-off valves and pressure switches. No published source gives field data for the assembly as a whole; its automatic valves and regulator match the US Army survey classes "Valve Operator" (C17-100 Electric, C17-300 Pneumatic) and "Pressure Control Assembly" (C8-000).

Published MTBF for the class ranges from 885,794 to 1,829,941 calendar hours.

Published MTTR for the class ranges from 3.00 to 18.42 hours.

## Basis

MTBF here counts calendar hours: the survey divides the calendar time over which data was collected by the number of failures. MTTR excludes logistics delay time.

## Source

US Army, TM 5-698-5, Survey of Reliability and Availability Information for Power Distribution, Power Generation, and HVAC Components for Commercial, Industrial, and Utility Installations, 22 July 2006, Appendix B, pages B-9 and B-13. https://www.wbdg.org/FFC/ARMYCOE/COETM/tm_5_698_5.pdf

The survey's own caveat: its figures establish a baseline for comparison and are not necessarily the results a specific facility will see.
