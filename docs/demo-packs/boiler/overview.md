# Boiler room: plant overview

Demo plant: Riverside Works boiler room. The plant, its records and its people are fictional; the room is the bundled boiler-room capture.

## What the plant makes

The boiler room raises treated feedwater to steam for the works, one batch at a time.

The unit of work is one batch: 500 L of treated feedwater, pumped to the header, raised to steam in a boiler and released after the control-panel check.

## Process

Batches come in at the intake and wait on a staging rack. They go through the feed pumps, queue at the header, and are heated in the boilers. After the boilers they wait in an inspection hold, are checked at the control panel, and leave at dispatch.

| Step | Machine | Units |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Transfer to the header | Feed pumps | 2 |
| Raise to steam | Boilers | 2 |
| Release check | Control panel | 1 |
| Fuel gas to the burners | Gas valve trains (support) | 2 |

## Operating pattern

The room runs one 8-hour shift a day, 5 days a week. The boilers cannot fire while either gas valve train is out of service.

## Condition of the fleet

The equipment is old and heavily used. Breakdowns are frequent and are logged as work orders in each machine's stoppage log. The logs, not the makers' or published figures, describe how this plant actually runs.

## Documents in this pack

Each machine has a stoppage log (this plant's own record) and a reference sheet (published figures for its class of equipment). The feed pumps, boilers and control panel also have the maker's datasheet.
