# Feed pumps: reference sheet

Published reliability figures for the equipment class. This sheet describes a class of equipment, not this plant.

## Equipment class

Boiler feedwater centrifugal pump, motor driven. Matched to the US Army survey class "Pump / Centrifugal" (H26-100) and its sub-classes "Integral Drive" (H26-110) and "Without Drive" (H26-120).

## Published figures

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |

| Without Drive (H26-120) | 711.3 | 3 | 2,076,992 | 5.67 | 0.999997272 |

Published MTBF for the class ranges from 1,166,025 to 2,076,992 calendar hours.

Published MTTR for the class ranges from 5.67 to 7.40 hours.

## Basis

MTBF here counts calendar hours: the survey divides the calendar time over which data was collected by the number of failures. MTTR excludes logistics delay time.

## Source

US Army, TM 5-698-5, Survey of Reliability and Availability Information for Power Distribution, Power Generation, and HVAC Components for Commercial, Industrial, and Utility Installations, 22 July 2006, Appendix B, page B-9. https://www.wbdg.org/FFC/ARMYCOE/COETM/tm_5_698_5.pdf

The survey's own caveat: its figures establish a baseline for comparison and are not necessarily the results a specific facility will see.
