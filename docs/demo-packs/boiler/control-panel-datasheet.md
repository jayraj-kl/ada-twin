# Control panel: datasheet

Meridew Controls · Model MC-12 boiler house control and burner-management panel · Datasheet DS-MC12, revision A

## Duty

Free-standing control cabinet with burner management, water-level controls and the batch release check, one panel installed.

## Performance per batch

Release check: one batch checked and released or held.

Cycle time: 120 s per batch.

Cycle-time variation (standard deviation divided by mean): 0.30.

Batches held and rejected at the check: 2% of batches.

## Notes

The check is done by the shift operator at the panel; the cycle time includes logging the batch.
