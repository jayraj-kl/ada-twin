# Boilers: reference sheet

Published reliability figures for the equipment class. This sheet describes a class of equipment, not this plant.

## Equipment class

Industrial steam boiler, gas fired. Matched to the US Army survey class "Boiler / Steam" (H8-200), with sub-classes "High Pressure" (H8-210) and "Low Pressure" (H8-220).

## Published figures

| Class (item) | Unit-years | Failures | MTBF (h) | MTTR (h) | Inherent availability |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Steam (H8-200) | 754.6 | 129 | 51,245.58 | 47.96 | 0.999064090 |
| Steam, High Pressure (H8-210) | 468.6 | 35 | 117,277.7 | 44.63 | 0.999619462 |
| Steam, Low Pressure (H8-220) | 286.1 | 94 | 26,659.1 | 49.20 | 0.998154400 |

Published MTBF for the class ranges from 26,659.1 to 117,277.7 calendar hours.

Published MTTR for the class ranges from 44.63 to 49.20 hours.

## Basis

MTBF here counts calendar hours: the survey divides the calendar time over which data was collected by the number of failures. MTTR excludes logistics delay time.

## Source

US Army, TM 5-698-5, Survey of Reliability and Availability Information for Power Distribution, Power Generation, and HVAC Components for Commercial, Industrial, and Utility Installations, 22 July 2006, Appendix B, page B-2. https://www.wbdg.org/FFC/ARMYCOE/COETM/tm_5_698_5.pdf

The survey's own caveat: its figures establish a baseline for comparison and are not necessarily the results a specific facility will see.
