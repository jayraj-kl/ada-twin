# Control panel: reference sheet

Published reliability figures for the equipment class. This sheet describes a class of equipment, not this plant.

## Equipment class

Boiler control panel and burner-management cabinet. Matched to the US Army survey classes "Control Panel" (C4-000) and, for a cabinet built around a programmable controller, "Control System" (C12-000).

Published MTBF for the class ranges from 10,345.28 to 1,647,876 calendar hours.

Published MTTR for the class ranges from 1.27 to 3.68 hours.

## Basis

MTBF here counts calendar hours: the survey divides the calendar time over which data was collected by the number of failures. MTTR excludes logistics delay time.

## Source

US Army, TM 5-698-5, Survey of Reliability and Availability Information for Power Distribution, Power Generation, and HVAC Components for Commercial, Industrial, and Utility Installations, 22 July 2006, Appendix B, pages B-5 and B-9. https://www.wbdg.org/FFC/ARMYCOE/COETM/tm_5_698_5.pdf

The survey's own caveat: its figures establish a baseline for comparison and are not necessarily the results a specific facility will see.
