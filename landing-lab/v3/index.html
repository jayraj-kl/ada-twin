Six steps, one page each.

From photos of your floor to a ranked list of changes. Each step keeps what the one before it found.

  1. Capture

    Upload 360° panoramas of your floor. World Labs Marble turns them into a walkable 3D world.

  2. Twin

    List your machines and draw how material flows between them. Gemini drafts the flow and finds the machines in your photos.

  3. Scenario

    Set the conditions in plain words: a heatwave, a rush order, half the maintenance team off sick.

  4. Agents

    Put a crew on the floor: a planner, technicians and a supervisor, each with a personality.

  5. Results

    Watch the crew decide in the 3D world and compare it with standard rules over many simulated days. Run picked changes on a copy of the twin.

  6. New plant

    See the applied changes ranked by how much they matter, drawn into photos of your plant by Nano Banana.

One sample run.

Boiler room, “Hot week, full order book”: an 8-hour shift at 34 °C with 340 batches on order. The crew of Priya, Marek and Sam works it once in the twin. Its playbook and standard rules, the fixed way of running the floor with no agents, then work the same shift on ten seeded days.

Gain per shift, paired day by day with the baseline on the same ten days. The picks then run as a trial on a copy of the twin, and only tested picks can be applied. sample run

Every change meets the same days.

The simulation is deterministic for a given seed. Day one replays the run’s seed and each further day takes the next, ten days by default and up to sixty. One lucky day cannot pass for the plant’s real performance.
