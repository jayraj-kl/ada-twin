Six steps on one path.

A project moves from photos of your floor to a ranked list of changes, one page per step. Each step keeps what the one before it found.

  1. Capture

    Upload 360° panoramas of your floor. World Labs Marble turns them into a walkable 3D world.

    world.spz · collider.glb · pano.png

  2. Twin

    List your machines and draw how material flows between them. Gemini drafts the flow from your description and finds the machines in your photos.

    boiler-1 → boiler-2 → boiler-3

  3. Scenario

    Set the conditions to test against, in plain words: a heatwave, a rush order, half the maintenance team off sick.

    8 h · 34 °C · 340 batches

  4. Agents

    Put a crew on the floor: a planner, technicians and a supervisor, each with a personality.

    Priya · Marek · Sam

  5. Results

    Run the shift and watch the agents decide in the 3D world. Compare them with standard rules over many simulated days, then pick changes, run them on a copy of your twin, and apply the ones that work.

    10 days · seeds 1–10

  6. New plant

    See the applied changes ranked by how much they matter, with Nano Banana drawing them into photos of your plant.

    gains +19 · +8 · +5 · +3 · sample run

What a run shows.

The crew works one shift in the twin. Its playbook and standard rules, the fixed way of running the floor with no agents, then work the same shift on ten seeded days. Sample run: boiler room, “Hot week, full order book”.

The same days, every time.

The simulation is deterministic for a given seed. Day one replays the run’s seed and each further day takes the next, so standard rules, the crew and every change meet exactly the same days. Ten days by default, up to sixty.
