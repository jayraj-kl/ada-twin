Six steps from photo to plan.

A project moves one way, like work through a line. The upper rung is what you do. The lower rung is what AdaTwin does with it.

  1. Capture

    You upload panoramas.

    World Labs Marble builds a walkable 3D world. world.spz

  2. Twin

    You name the machines.

    Gemini drafts the flow and finds the machines in your photos. boiler-1…4

  3. Scenario

    You describe a heatwave, a rush order, staff off sick.

    Gemini turns it into a shift to test. 8 h · 34 °C

  4. Agents

    You pick a planner, technicians and a supervisor.

    Each agent gets a role and a personality. Priya · Marek · Sam

  5. Results

    You pick changes to try.

    The crew is compared with standard rules over many days, and picks run on a copy of the twin. 10 days

  6. New plant

    You apply what works.

    Nano Banana draws the changes into photos of your plant. boiler-2 · +19 · sample run

The crew against standard rules.

The crew works the shift once in the twin. Its playbook and standard rules, the fixed way of running the floor with no agents, then work the same shift on every seeded day, so the comparison is fair. This is one sample run of the boiler room on “Hot week, full order book”.

Same seed, same day.

The simulation is deterministic for a given seed. Day one replays the run’s seed and each further day takes the next, so every change is measured against exactly the same days. The voxels at the top of this page are drawn from seed 1 too, and they land in the same place on every visit.
